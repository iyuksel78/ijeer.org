About IJEER

IJEER is an international scholarly journal for high-quality research across educational contexts. We welcome empirical studies, systematic reviews, conceptual work, and research-informed perspectives.

Our editorial approach prioritizes methodological clarity, ethical research practice, readable reporting, and contributions with practical or theoretical value.
