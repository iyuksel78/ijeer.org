What IJEER Publishes

IJEER publishes a broad range of article types, focusing on any educational issue, including:

  • Original Research Articles (quantitative, qualitative, mixed-methods)
  • Systematic Reviews, Scoping Reviews, and Meta-Analyses
  • Conceptual and Theoretical Papers
  • Policy and Practice Briefs
  • Methods Notes and Short Reports
  • Book Reviews and Scholarly Opinion Essays

Our Values

IJEER is guided by four core commitments:

  1. Rigor: clear designs, robust analysis, and transparent reporting
  2. Relevance: research that informs practice, policy, and improvement
  3. Equity: inclusive perspectives and diverse educational contexts
  4. Integrity: strong ethics, responsible authorship, and fair review

Open Access and Publication Schedule

IJEER is published twice a year (June and December). The journal is fully open access and free of charge for both authors and readers. All manuscripts undergo double-blind peer review.

Language Policy

Submissions are accepted primarily in English. Special issues may include full texts in other languages when accompanied by an extended English abstract, subject to editorial approval.

Call for Papers

IJEER welcomes original, high-quality manuscripts that contribute to educational research, policy, practice, and innovation.
