The journal

About IJEER

International Journal of Educational Excellence and Research (IJEER) is an international, peer-reviewed, open-access journal dedicated to strengthening high-quality scholarship on educational excellence and evidence-informed educational improvement.

The journal welcomes original research articles and rigorous review articles addressing contemporary challenges in teaching, learning, leadership, assessment, and educational systems.

IJEER values interdisciplinary dialogue and encourages contributions that broaden global perspectives, especially research emerging from underrepresented regions and contexts.

The journal

Aims & Scope

IJEER publishes scholarly work that advances understanding of what educational excellence means, how it can be measured, and how it can be achieved across different systems, cultures, and learning environments.

Core aims

  • Strengthen evidence-informed practice and policy in education.
  • Promote innovative and responsible research that improves learning and teaching.
  • Support inclusive scholarship reflecting diverse educational contexts.
  • Connect research with real-world improvement through interdisciplinary dialogue.

Scope

Learning & assessmentTeaching, learning sciences, curriculum, evaluation, and psychometrics.
Systems & leadershipGovernance, policy, leadership, and improvement systems.
Equity & wellbeingInclusion, culturally responsive education, and wellbeing.
Technology & developmentDigital learning, AI-supported education, and teacher education.

For authors

Author Guidelines

Before you submit

Submissions must be original, not under review elsewhere, methodologically sound, ethically approved where required, and aligned with the journal’s aims and scope. References and style should follow APA 7.

Word-count guidance

  • Research article: 6,000–9,000 words
  • Review article: 6,000–10,000 words

Required submission package

  1. Anonymous manuscript without author-identifying information.
  2. Title page with names, affiliations, ORCIDs, and corresponding-author details.
  3. Funding, conflicts of interest, ethics, consent, AI-use, and data-availability declarations.
  4. Clearly labelled figures and tables; supplementary files where relevant.

Structured abstract

A 150–250 word abstract covering background, purpose, design or context, data or measures, analytic approach, results, and implications is strongly encouraged.

Reporting and transparency

Authors should follow the reporting guideline appropriate to their study design and provide data, materials, code, or a justified availability statement where applicable.

For reviewers

Review for IJEER

Reviewers provide objective, constructive, and confidential evaluations supporting the journal’s academic quality. Reviews should consider scope fit, originality, methodological quality, ethical integrity, clarity, and contribution.

Confidentiality and conflicts

Reviewers must treat manuscripts and review materials as confidential, disclose any competing interest before accepting an invitation, and must not use unpublished information for personal advantage. Generative AI tools must not receive confidential manuscript content.

Researchers interested in reviewing may contact the editorial office with their areas of expertise, affiliation, and ORCID.

Policies

Peer Review

All suitable manuscripts undergo double-blind peer review designed to support scholarly quality, impartiality, confidentiality, and fair editorial decisions.

Review path

  1. Editorial screening: scope, originality, completeness, ethical requirements, and basic scholarly quality.
  2. External review: normally at least two independent reviewers assess suitable manuscripts.
  3. Decision: accept, minor revision, major revision, resubmit, or reject. The editor makes the final decision and may seek additional review.
  4. Revision and verification: authors respond point by point; revised work may return to reviewers.
  5. Final checks: authorship, disclosures, formatting, and publication files are verified before publication.

Editors and reviewers must declare competing interests. A submission authored by an editor is handled independently, without that editor’s access to the decision process.

Policies

Publication Ethics

IJEER follows principles of responsible scholarly publishing and considers concerns fairly, confidentially, and with appropriate opportunity to respond.

Authorship and originality

Listed authors must have made substantial contributions, approved the submitted version, and accepted accountability for the work. Plagiarism, duplicate submission, fabricated or falsified data, citation manipulation, and undisclosed conflicts are prohibited. Submissions may be screened for text similarity.

Human and animal research

Research involving people, identifiable data, or animals must report appropriate ethics approval, consent, and safeguards, or explain why approval was not required.

Competing interests, funding, and data

Authors must disclose financial and non-financial competing interests, funding and sponsor roles, and data availability. Editors and reviewers must recuse themselves when impartial assessment is not possible.

Artificial intelligence

AI tools cannot be listed as authors. Authors must disclose substantive AI assistance and remain responsible for accuracy, originality, citations, confidentiality, and interpretation. Reviewers and editors must not upload confidential manuscripts to external AI systems.

Corrections, retractions, and concerns

Material errors may be addressed through a correction, expression of concern, or retraction. Decisions are based on the reliability of the scholarly record rather than punishment. Concerns should be sent to editor@ijeer.org.

Complaints and appeals

Authors may submit a reasoned appeal identifying a procedural or factual error. A person not responsible for the original decision will review the appeal where practicable. Complaints about process, conduct, or conflicts are acknowledged and examined separately from the manuscript’s academic merits.

Policies

Privacy Notice

IJEER processes names, contact details, affiliations, manuscript files, declarations, reviewer records, correspondence, and technical security data only to administer submission, peer review, publication, integrity checks, and the scholarly record.

Access and disclosure

Access is limited to authorized editors, reviewers where appropriate, technical service providers, and others when required by law or research-integrity procedures. Reviewer and author identities are handled according to the double-blind process.

Retention

Submission metadata and decision correspondence may be retained as part of the editorial record. Files from rejected or withdrawn submissions are normally removed from active storage within 12 months unless they are needed for an appeal, ethics inquiry, legal obligation, or documented author request. Published records are preserved as part of the scholarly record.

Your requests

To request access, correction, or deletion where compatible with scholarly-record and legal obligations, contact editor@ijeer.org. Do not include passwords or unnecessary sensitive data.
